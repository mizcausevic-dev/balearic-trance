# Balearic Trance is a note-only MIDI construction kit

Four open kits, chord names taken from the keys, and a motif the return restates. The preview is an excerpt, not a master

The pack is four coordinated kits in 4/4. Tempos are 128, 126, 132, 136 BPM, one fixed integer per kit, inside the 125 to 138 range used for this project. Each kit has six core parts: drums, bass, harmonic keys, motif, sequence, and pad. Every core part has two variations. That is 48 files. There is no reference-only count. Part files are 8 bars. Each kit also has one 32-bar arrangement.

The kits are Salt Terrace in D Mixolydian, White Cove in A major, Glass Room in G Lydian, Ferry Light in F major. k01.motif.a opens F#5 to A5, a minor third. k02.motif.a opens C#5 to A4, a major third. k03.motif.a opens B4 to E5, a perfect fourth. k04.motif.a opens F5 to G5, a major second. The motif themes are different melodies. They are not transpositions of one line with the same velocities. Variation B of each part changes start times, pitches, and durations. Velocity alone is not the difference.

Each arrangement has four 8-bar sections. Bars 1-8 state the theme: drums, bass, harmonic keys, motif, and pad, with the sequence silent. Bars 9-16 develop the sequence over drums, bass, keys, and pad. The motif does not play in that section. Bars 17-24 are the open section: harmonic keys, a sparse sequence, and a pad, with no drums, no bass, and no motif. Bars 25-32 are the return. The return places the same motif A part as the theme, with the same drums, bass, keys, and pad. The motif is restated. It is not a new tune.
